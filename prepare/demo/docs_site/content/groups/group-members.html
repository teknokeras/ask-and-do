Use the members endpoints to change who is in a group one person at a time. To set the full member list in one call, see Replace a group.

Add a member

curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8000/v1/groups/grp_7d2e91b0/members \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"user_id": "usr_4f9a2c1e"}'

Adding someone who is already a member does nothing and returns 200. The user must be active; adding a deactivated user fails with 422 user_deactivated. People with a pending invitation cannot be added until they accept.

Remove a member

Removing a member takes the user out of this group only. They keep their account, their other groups, and the projects they own. They lose access to projects that they could only see through this group.

curl -s -X DELETE http://localhost:8000/v1/groups/grp_7d2e91b0/members/usr_4f9a2c1e \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN"

To take someone out of the whole organization, see Removing users from the organization. That endpoint is DELETE /v1/org/members/{user_id}.

List members

Any role can list a group's members. The list is paginated like every other list; see Pagination and filtering.

curl -s http://localhost:8000/v1/groups/grp_7d2e91b0/members \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN"

Who can change members

Only admins can add or remove members. Viewers get 403 forbidden, even for groups they belong to.
