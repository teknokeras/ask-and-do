An invitation asks a person to join your organization. They receive an email, choose their display name, and become an active user when they accept. Until then the invitation is pending and the person is not a user: they do not appear in GET /v1/users and cannot be added to groups.

To make an account right away without waiting for the person, create the user instead.

Send an invitation

Only admins can invite.

curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8000/v1/invitations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"email": "sam@example.com", "role": "viewer", "message": "Welcome to the team!"}'
FieldRequiredNotes
emailYesMust not belong to an existing user.
roleNoRole the person gets on accepting. Defaults to viewer.
messageNoShown in the email. Up to 500 characters.

Invitation lifecycle

An invitation has one of four statuses:

  • pending: sent, not yet accepted.
  • accepted: the person joined. Their new user ID is in user_id.
  • expired: not accepted within 7 days.
  • revoked: an admin cancelled it.

An organization can have at most 100 pending invitations at a time.

Resend an invitation

Resending emails the invitation again and resets its expiry to 7 days from now. You can resend an invitation up to 5 times. Expired invitations can be resent too; they become pending again.

curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8000/v1/invitations/inv_93ab01cd/resend \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN"

Revoke an invitation

Revoking stops the link from working. Accepted invitations cannot be revoked; deactivate or remove the user instead.

curl -s -X DELETE http://localhost:8000/v1/invitations/inv_93ab01cd \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN"

List invitations

Only admins can list invitations. Filter by status with ?status=pending.

curl -s "http://localhost:8000/v1/invitations?status=pending" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LARKSPUR_TOKEN"
